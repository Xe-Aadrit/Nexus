======= Nexus =======

Timezone: UTC (Coordinated Universal Time)

Features:

- Supports both Slash Commands ('/') and Prefix Commands ('!', etc.)

- Activity Tracking
    - Our bot tracks activity of members across chats and VCs (1 Day / 7 Days / 30 Days / n Days)
    - Chat Activity: No of messages sent
    - VC Activity:
        - Amount of time spent in a VC
        - Amount of time Streamed
        - Amount of time spent with microphone on
        - Amount of time spent speaking
        NOTE: Amount of time speaking is NOT equal to Amount of time spent with microphone on.
    - NOTE: No sensitive information is ever collected or stored.

- Elite Points
    - Every time when someone votes, the vote tracker of Top.gg sends a webhook. That webhook will be useful for automating the Elite Points distribution process every time someone votes.

- Automated Roles: VC, Chat, Streaming
    - Our bot tracks the activity of members across chats (no. of messages sent) and VCs. We'll use this data to automate the roles of:
        - Streamer Of The Week (Most Time Streamed)
        - VC Dominator (Most Time spent in a VC)
        - Soul Of The Chat (Most Messages Sent)
    - These are counted on a WEEKLY basis, giving every one a fair chance every week.

- Anti Spam & Anti Farm Systems (Toggleable)
    - Anti Spam System:
        - This system doesn't let users get xp for spam messages (multiple messages sent in a short duration of time.)
        - This is toggleable, i.e. it can be turned on/off.

    - Anti Farm System:
        - This system doesn't let users farm VC hours (primarily for VC Dominator role.)
        - This is toggleable, i.e. it can be turned on/off.
